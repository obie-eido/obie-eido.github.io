Falling Diamonds
Conceptual Brand Created In Taipei

Falling Diamonds was developed over six months in a fashion design course in Taiwan. The work blends graphic identity, garment design, pattern drafting, and narrative branding into a single system. The deck walks through early concept sketches, logo and wordmark exploration, fabric and silhouette ideas, and the visual language that holds the collection together.

The goal was to treat the brand as a story that can move from flat graphics to physical garments to images on a screen, without losing its mood or point of view. The final outcome is a tight set of visuals that can support lookbooks, line sheets, and campaign assets.

Lookbook Output
Falling Diamonds Photoshoot

The photoshoot translates the Falling Diamonds system into lived images, focusing on silhouette, texture, and mood. These frames were shot in Taipei and styled to echo the graphic language of the deck, so the brand story stays consistent from sketch to finished image.
