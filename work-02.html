Falling Diamonds
Conceptual Brand Created in Taipei

Falling Diamonds was developed over six months during a fashion design program in Taiwan. The project combines graphic design, technical garment construction, pattern drafting, brand development, and conceptual storytelling. The full deck includes early brand strategy slides, visual identity layouts, clothing construction references, and a full editorial-style photoshoot shot on location in Taipei. The project demonstrates an understanding of fashion branding, visual direction, fabrication logic, and narrative design.
